Product positioning

QuitGentle is for people who want practical smoking awareness without a judgmental tone. The app focuses on quick cigarette and craving logs, rescue flows like breathing and delay, progress history, optional hotspot insights, optional Cloud Sync, and clear privacy controls.

It is not clinical care, not a diagnosis tool, not emergency support, and not a product that promises guaranteed quit success.
