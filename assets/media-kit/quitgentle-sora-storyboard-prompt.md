# Sora Storyboard Prompt

Attach every image inside `assets/logos` and `assets/ui_refs` before running this prompt.

## Prompt

Create a `25-second`, `9:16 vertical`, punchy mobile product ad for `QuitGentle Smoking Tracker`.

Use the attached images as hard visual references for the app icon, device UI, color palette, card styling, layout density, and overall product feel. When the phone screen is visible, the app should look like the attached QuitGentle references, not a generic made-up wellness app.

QuitGentle is a calm, local-first smoking cessation companion focused on fast logging, craving support, progress tracking, hotspot map insights, widgets, and Apple Watch support.

The ad should feel like a modern paid social mobile ad:

- fast hook in the first 2 seconds
- tight cuts
- tactile taps and swipes
- crisp phone close-ups
- subtle cinematic lighting
- grounded, high-contrast dark UI
- premium but practical, not glossy fantasy tech

The visual direction must stay close to these product truths:

- dark background UI
- ember, teal, and green accents
- rounded iPhone-native cards and controls
- quick cigarette logging
- quick craving logging
- rescue moments like breathing and delay timer
- hotspot map / place-based insight moments
- progress screen with streak and progress cues
- widget and Apple Watch support

Do not introduce:

- fake purple gradients
- bright white dashboard screens
- Android-looking controls
- floating holograms
- fake medical charts
- subscription paywalls
- account creation flows
- claims of guaranteed quit success

Show real-life usage context around the phone:

- morning coffee
- commute pause
- work-break craving
- quiet recovery moment

Keep the pacing strong, but the emotional tone calm and capable.

## Storyboard Beats

### Beat 1: Hook, 0s-3s

A stressed user reaches for their phone during a craving moment. Quick close-up, hand, coffee, shallow depth of field, fast unlock. Cut immediately to QuitGentle opening.

On-screen text:
`Craving again?`

### Beat 2: Fast Logging, 3s-7s

Show QuitGentle's Today or Log experience with a fast cigarette or craving log. Emphasize speed, clarity, and one-tap action. UI should closely resemble the attached references.

On-screen text:
`Open. Tap. Done.`

### Beat 3: Rescue, 7s-12s

Show the Rescue section. Include a craving-support moment like the breathing flow or delay timer. Keep the phone screen clear, dark, and faithful to the reference pack.

On-screen text:
`Calm support, right away`

### Beat 4: Pattern Insight, 12s-17s

Show a hotspot map or history-based insight moment. The app should feel like it helps the user understand where urges show up, not just count events.

On-screen text:
`See the pattern. Break the pattern.`

### Beat 5: Progress Proof, 17s-21s

Show progress tracking with streak/progress visuals, useful metrics, and a clear sense of momentum.

On-screen text:
`Track real progress`

### Beat 6: Ecosystem Finish, 21s-25s

Show quick flashes of widget and Apple Watch support, then end on a clean icon end card with the QuitGentle icon from the attached logo pack.

On-screen text:
`QuitGentle`

Secondary end-card text:
`Calm craving support and progress tracking`

## Voiceover

When a craving hits, you need help now. QuitGentle helps you log it fast, reset with calm rescue tools, spot your patterns, and keep moving forward. Track real progress, and keep support close on your phone, widgets, and Apple Watch.

## Shot Guidance

- Use clean iPhone hand-held product shots.
- Let at least 4 shots show the real app UI clearly on screen.
- Add tiny finger taps and swipe gestures to sell usability.
- Use subtle motion on UI, but do not morph the interface into something fictional.
- Prefer realistic reflections and screen glare over fake glass effects.
- Keep transitions quick and ad-friendly.

## Output Goal

Deliver a polished mobile ad concept that feels install-ready and performance-marketing friendly, while preserving QuitGentle's actual visual identity and product truth.
